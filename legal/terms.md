## What jipbap is

You tell it what is in your kitchen; it tells you what you can cook, what you
are an item or two short of, and what to buy. It keeps a grocery list and a
record of what you cooked.

It is a finding tool, not a cookbook. **We do not write the recipes.** For each
one we show a title, a source, an ingredient list and a link. The method lives
on the site that published it, and that is where we send you.

Your privacy is covered by the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## The allergen and dietary filters are not a safety check

jipbap can hide recipes containing things you have excluded. **Do not rely on it
if getting it wrong would hurt you.**

- Our ingredient data is machine-assigned and only partly reviewed. It has
  errors.
- An ingredient list is not a label. "Gochujang" does not tell us whether that
  jar contains wheat. The jar in your hand does.
- Cross-contamination, shared equipment and reformulated products are invisible
  to anything we can see.

**If an ingredient can hurt you, read the packet.** This filter narrows a list.
It does not clear anything to eat.

Likewise, *"you have everything"* is a claim about what you told us is in your
kitchen, not about whether the jar is empty or the scallions turned.
